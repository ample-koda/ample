## The Advanced window

Every effect has an **Advanced** window (the button on its card and in its properties). It is a floating window with the same controls as AMPLE Jam: drag it by the title bar, roll it up to the title bar with the **-** button (or double-click the title bar), make it fill the screen with the square button (Esc brings it back, a second Esc closes it) and resize it from the corner; it remembers its place per block and its size per kind of block.

- **The power switch and the status** in the title bar are two separate things. The round power button (tooltip: *Block on / bypassed*) switches the block on or off; off means bypassed, the sound passes through. The text next to it only informs: **Live preview** (a change of a knob is heard at once while the sound plays), **Needs a build** (a change is heard after the block is built again) or **Bypassed**. Hover the text for the explanation; it cannot be clicked.
- **The menu** (the three lines) holds Snapshots (store four settings of the block, A to D, and morph between them), Reset to defaults (one undo step), Help (this block's page in these docs) and Copy block code.
- **The toolbar** under the title bar has Play, Loop and A/B, the waveform, and Compare / render for the exact render of the block.
- **The knobs** show their name and value under the knob; a small dot in the corner says whether the knob moves live (hover it for the details). The explanations of a panel are behind the small **i** in the title bar; graphs fill the width of the window at any size, and the (i) also tells which parameters move live. On a phone the window fills the screen and keeps the menu.

The music side of each of these is explained, with sounds, in the Music track: [space and depth](#/learn/music/advanced/space-and-depth), [compression](#/learn/music/advanced/dynamics), [frequencies and EQ](#/learn/music/advanced/eq-and-frequencies) and [movement](#/learn/music/expert/modulation).
