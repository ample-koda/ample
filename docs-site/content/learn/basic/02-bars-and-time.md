<h2 id="b">Beat and bar</h2><p>A <b>beat</b> is the pulse you tap your foot to. Most songs count four beats in a <b>bar</b> (4/4). <code>tempo 96</code> is 96 beats per minute.</p>
<h2 id="a">At and for</h2><p><code>at 1 for 8</code> means: from bar 1, for 8 bars. A block lives in a stretch of bars.</p>{{pv {"title":"One bar of four beats","src":"drums","fx":"dry","p":{},"note":"Kick on 1 and 3, snare on 2 and 4, hi-hat on the eighth notes."}}}
