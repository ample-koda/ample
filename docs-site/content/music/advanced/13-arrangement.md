## Layers

A full song has roles:

- **Rhythm**: drums and percussion.
- **Bass**: the low floor.
- **Harmony**: chords (pads, guitar, piano).
- **Melody**: the line you hum.
- **Texture**: small extras (shakers, noise, effects).

## Add and remove

Build tension by adding layers; release it by removing them. A drop in a dance track is mostly a sudden removal followed by a return.

## Leave space

If everything plays all the time, nothing stands out. Give each part its moment.

{{deeper Who plays when: an example of a simple arrangement}}
| Section | Drums | Bass | Chords | Melody |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intro | - | - | pad | - |
| Verse | soft | yes | pad | voice |
| Chorus | full | yes | full chords | voice + doubling |
| Bridge | - | pedal | thin | new line |
| Last chorus | full | yes | full + extra layer | voice + harmony |
{{/deeper}}

{{deeper Common mistakes}}
- **Everything plays all the time.** Remove something in the verse so the chorus has somewhere to go.
- **Two sounds in the same register and role.** Give them different jobs or ranges.
- **Too many ideas.** One strong riff beats five weak ones.
- **No ending.** Decide how the song stops (fade, hit, silence) before you start.
{{/deeper}}
