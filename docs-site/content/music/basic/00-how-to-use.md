## How it works

Every page has three kinds of things:

- **Text** that explains one idea in plain words.
- **Sounds you can play**: press the round play button. Many have a **Dry / With effect** switch, so you can hear the difference.
- **Boxes you can open**: "Go deeper" is for when you want the why. "Try it" is a small listening exercise.

> Listen first, read second. Music is something you hear. If a page makes no sense yet, play its sounds twice and read again.

## The path

1. **Basic**: pulse, pitch, notes, scales, chords. After this you can *hear* what a song is made of.
2. **Advanced**: how parts fit together: bass, chord progressions, structure, EQ, space.
3. **Expert**: more harmony, sound design and voices.
4. **Master**: mixing, mastering and training your ears.

There is also a [glossary](#/learn/music/master/glossary) for every word that sounds like jargon.

{{deeper How to practise so it sticks}}
Learning music is mostly learning to hear. A few habits help more than long sessions:

- **Short and often.** Ten minutes a day beats two hours once a week. Your ears learn while you rest.
- **Sing it.** Even badly. If you can hum a rhythm or a note, you understand it. Humming connects hearing to doing.
- **Name what you hear.** "The second chord feels tense." Words give the sound a handle, so you remember it.
- **Come back.** The second time through a page you hear things you missed. Nothing here is a test.
- **One new idea at a time.** If a page introduces three terms, master the first before the second.

If you get stuck: play the preview, then read the paragraph above it again. The sound is the truth; the text is only a label for it.
{{/deeper}}

{{deeper What you will be able to do at each stage}}
| After | You can |
|---|---|
| Basic | tap a beat, tell major from minor, name notes on a keyboard, understand what a bar and a chord are |
| Advanced | write a four-chord loop with a bass and drums, hear why a part sounds muddy, shape a sound with EQ, reverb and compression |
| Expert | colour harmony with modes and borrowed chords, build a synth sound from scratch, make textures |
| Master | mix a song so every part is heard, finish it at a sensible loudness, judge your own work |
