## Which one is higher?

Play two notes. Do not think about the name, only decide: up or down?

{{pv {"title":"Example A","src":"seq","p":{"notes":[60,64],"step":1}}}}
{{pv {"title":"Example B","src":"seq","p":{"notes":[64,60],"step":1}}}}

{{deeper Answer}}
Example A goes **up** (C to E, a major third). Example B goes **down** (E to C). If you were unsure, play them again and follow the pitch with your hand: up for higher, down for lower.
{{/deeper}}

## Happy or sad

Listen to a chord and decide if it is major or minor.

{{pv {"title":"Chord 1","src":"chords","p":{"chords":[[60,63,67]],"step":2,"wave":"triangle"}}}}
{{pv {"title":"Chord 2","src":"chords","p":{"chords":[[60,64,67]],"step":2,"wave":"triangle"}}}}

{{deeper Answer}}
Chord 1 is **minor** (C minor: C, E flat, G; the third is three semitones above the root) and sounds darker or sadder. Chord 2 is **major** (C major: C, E, G; the third is four semitones) and sounds brighter. Play them one after the other and listen for exactly that change in the middle note.
{{/deeper}}

## A/B with level matching

Use the **Dry / With effect** switch. Our ears prefer louder sounds, so make sure the two versions have the same level before you decide which is better.

{{try A daily five minutes}}
Pick one thing per day: an interval, a chord, an effect. Listen to a short example, name it, check. After a few weeks you will know the sounds by ear.
{{/try}}

{{deeper Intervals by song}}
Associate each interval with a tune you know. Upward: minor second (Jaws), major second (Happy Birthday), minor third (Greensleeves), major third (When the Saints), perfect fourth (Here Comes the Bride), perfect fifth (Twinkle Twinkle), octave (Somewhere over the Rainbow). The songs differ for each person, so choose ones that you know well.
{{/deeper}}

{{deeper Training frequencies}}
Take one sound and boost a band with an EQ, then cut it, and say what changed. Do this daily at 250 Hz (boxy), 1 kHz (honky), 3 kHz (harsh), 8 kHz (sizzle). After a few weeks you can often name the band that is wrong. This is the fastest way to speed up mixing.
