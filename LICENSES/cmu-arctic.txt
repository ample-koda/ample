The Flite voices slt, rms and awb (the recordings of the CMU ARCTIC speech databases)

The Flite clustergen voices cmu_us_slt (US English, female), cmu_us_rms (US English, male) and cmu_us_awb (Scottish English, male) were built by
Carnegie Mellon University from recordings of the speakers slt, rms and awb. AMPLE converts the generated data of these three voices into a compact
binary form (src/Ample.Speech/Flite/Cg/Data). The conversion is a modification: it does not change a value, and it is checked against the compiled
Flite program. The converted data is not endorsed by Carnegie Mellon University or by the authors of Flite or of the ARCTIC databases.
