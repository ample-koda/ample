# Versions: studio and garage

AMPLE remembers your work. Every time you save, a new **revision** is added to the history, and you can always go back. On top of that, AMPLE gives you two places to work: the **garage** and the **studio**.

- The **garage** is where you work. Every save lands here, so you can try anything.
- The **studio** is the main version: the one you would call "the real song". It only changes when you decide.

The little labels in the header tell you where you are: **on: garage** is the line you are working on, and **★ studio** shows the revision the main version points at.

## Checkpoints

A **checkpoint** is a snapshot that you take yourself, with a name, before something risky. Press **Checkpoint** in the header, name it, and you can come back to exactly this moment later. A checkpoint changes nothing else.

## The History panel

Open the **History** tab at the bottom. It draws every revision as a dot on a line. The ring marks **here** (where you are) and the star marks the **studio**.

![[shot:versions-history|The History panel. The two buttons at the left are explained below.]]

At the top left of the panel are the two buttons that move the studio:

- **Make this the main version** (promote): the studio becomes what the garage is now. Before it does anything, AMPLE lists exactly what will change. The old studio stays in the history, so nothing is lost.
- **Start a new try** (fork): start a new line of work from the main version, as a new garage. What you had on the old garage stays in the history.

> **Careful** Only one garage is "the" garage at a time. After you promote, an older garage is no longer shown in the daily view; it is still in the history if you ever need it.

## Looking without touching

Double-click a revision to go there, or choose **Look only** to see an old state without changing anything: AMPLE then says "Read-only" and refuses to build or save until you leave.
