# The arrangement

The **Arrangement** tab shows the whole song as a timeline. Where the node editor shows how blocks are connected, the arrangement shows **when** each of them plays.

![[shot:arrangement-range|The Arrangement. Dragging over the ruler (2) chooses some bars; the button (1) plays just those.]]

## Reading it

- The **ruler** at the top counts the bars, with the seconds beside them. The tempo decides how many seconds a bar takes.
- Every block with sound or notes has a **row**. The coloured bar in a row is the stretch of bars in which that block plays: its `at` and `for`, or its named parts.
- Below the ruler, when the song is built, the **waveform** of the whole song is drawn. Click in it to jump to that moment.
- **− and +** change how wide a bar is. A block you right-click offers **Open in source**, which jumps to its text.

## Listening to a few bars

1. Press the mouse on the ruler and drag to the right: a band shows the chosen bars.
2. Press the button that says, for example, **preview bars 4-8** (or the **Play bars** button in the header).
3. AMPLE builds just that stretch if it has to, and plays it.

This is the quickest way to work on one place of a song: choose the bars, listen, change something, listen again. Turn on **loop** to hear them over and over.

> **Tip** Chosen bars are only for listening. They never change the song or the build you keep.

## Recording and importing audio

Two buttons at the top of the tab bring sound in from outside:

- **Record…** records from the microphone. You can listen to it first, and then add the take to the project as a file.
- **Separate and transcribe audio…** takes a recording and tries to split it into parts and write down the notes in it. It uses a local analysis tool that has to be installed on your computer, and it stays on your machine.
